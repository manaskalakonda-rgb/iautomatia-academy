## 1. Executive summary

## 2. Problem and opportunity

**The problem.** Plants in automotive, electronics, pharma and smart manufacturing are automating fast, but they struggle to hire engineers who can work on real equipment from day one. Graduates of core branches (Mech, EEE, ECE, EIE, Mechatronics) usually leave college with theory, and sometimes with a simulator-based PLC course, but they have:

- never wired or fault-found a real PLC,
- never programmed or safely operated an industrial or collaborative robot,
- never connected a PLC to a robot over an industrial network, and
- used only one PLC brand, while plants run a mix.

Employers therefore spend months on on-the-job training, and graduates who could fill automation roles stay under-employed.

**The opportunity.** Collaborative robots have lowered the cost and space needed for real robotics training, and fence-less cobots can be used safely in a classroom-scale lab. An institute that combines **cobots + multi-brand PLCs + vision + industrial networks on real hardware**, with small batches and placement support, fills a gap that neither low-cost PLC institutes nor brand-locked OEM centres currently cover in Hyderabad.

## 3. Solution and programs

A hardware-first, job-oriented curriculum in three stackable levels. Each level includes everything in the previous one.

| Program | Duration | Hours | Fee (excl. GST) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | 4 weeks | ~80 | Rs 35,000 | PLC basics (ladder logic, I/O, timers/counters), cobot safety, jogging, teach pendant, basic pick-and-place |
| **Integrator** | 8 weeks | ~160 | Rs 65,000 | Foundation + PLC–cobot communication over Modbus TCP and Profinet, HMI basics, vision-guided pick-and-place, grippers and end-of-arm tooling |
| **Specialist** | 12 weeks | ~240 | Rs 95,000 | Integrator + complete automation cell build, multi-brand PLC (Siemens TIA Portal, Mitsubishi GX Works, Allen-Bradley Studio 5000), fault diagnosis, capstone project, interview prep and placement support |

**Delivery**
- Batch size 12; at most 4 students per cobot cell (3 cells = one batch).
- Weekday batches (morning, afternoon, evening) and weekend batches for working engineers.
- Certificate from iAutomatia Academy; a co-branded OEM certificate is **planned** (not yet agreed).
- EMI options available.
- Capstone project mentoring and career/placement support (support, not a job guarantee).

**Target roles for graduates:** Automation Engineer, PLC Programmer, Robotics Technician, Controls Engineer.

## 5. Competition and positioning

| Competitor type | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| **PLC-only institutes** (Ameerpet / Kukatpally) | Cheap, many locations, well known | Simulator-heavy, usually one PLC brand, no cobots or vision, large batches |
| **OEM training centres** | Brand credibility, official certification | Brand-locked, costly, short courses, not job-oriented |
| **Online courses** | Low price, flexible | No hardware at all |
| **Government skill missions** | Free or subsidised, wide reach | Generic curricula, limited robotics hardware, variable placement links |

**Our edge**
- **Multi-brand PLC + cobot + vision on real hardware**: one integrated cell rather than isolated topics.
- **Small ratios**: 12 per batch, max 4 per cobot cell.
- **Capstone project**: a working automation cell that graduates can show employers.
- **Placement support** with system integrators and plants.

**Positioning:** priced above PLC-only institutes and well below OEM centres, justified by hands-on hardware hours. *"Program real robots, not simulators."*

## 7. Operations

**Facility:** ~2,000 sq ft in [Area], Hyderabad, with 3 cobot cells, 6 PLC trainer kits (2 each: Siemens, Mitsubishi, Allen-Bradley), 3 vision sensors, laptops and a classroom and counselling area.

**Capacity (planning assumption)**
- 3 cells × 4 students = 12 seats per slot; 4 slots per week (morning, afternoon, evening, weekend) = **48 seats in training at any time**.
- Average program length (by mix) ≈ 7.4 weeks, so at the Year-1 exit rate of 28 enrolments/month the lab is at about **100% utilisation by December 2027**. This is why the **Sep 2027 decision on cells 4–5** is critical: cells must be ordered and installed by early 2028.
- Y2 (5 cells, 80 seats) about 75% average utilisation; Y3 (6 cells, 96 seats) about 80%.

**Team (Year 1)**
- 1 lead trainer + 3 trainers (Rs 3.45 L/month)
- Centre manager + 2 counsellors / placement executives (Rs 1.5 L/month)
- Trainers certified on JAKA cobots and the three PLC platforms before launch.

**Key processes**
- Standard lesson plans and lab sheets for every week; practical assessments at weeks 4, 8 and 12.
- Preventive maintenance and an annual maintenance contract (AMC) with JAKA; one cell can act as a spare during breakdowns.
- Safety induction for every learner; cobot safety settings reviewed before each batch.
- CRM for enquiries, demos, enrolments, EMI and placement tracking.

- Revenue is counted in the month of enrolment. EMI timing, GST and taxes are not modelled.

### 8.5 Cash and payback (from the model)

- Monthly EBITDA is negative for January–May 2027; the cumulative low point is about **−Rs 16.5 L (May 2027)**. Working capital of Rs 25 L covers this, with the lowest cash balance at about **Rs 8.5 L**.

- **Watch-out:** pre-launch operating costs (Oct–Dec 2026 rent, trainer salaries during certification) are **not** in the capex list. At roughly 2 months of partial opex they could use Rs 8–12 L of the working-capital buffer. Either budget them explicitly or raise the working-capital line.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow enrolments** | Break-even slips past June 2027; cash buffer shrinks | Campus demos with the portable cell, EMI options, free workshops, college tie-ups; monthly tracking of the enquiry → demo → enrolment funnel |
| **Trainer attrition** | Quality drops, batches delayed | Certification path for trainers, retention bonus, documented lesson plans so trainers can be replaced |
| **Hardware downtime** | Lost lab hours, unhappy learners | AMC with JAKA, spare cell capacity, preventive maintenance, spare parts on site |
| **Weak placements** | Reputation and referrals suffer | Sign hiring partners **before launch**, capstone portfolios, interview prep, publish only verified outcomes |
| **Price competition** | Pressure on fees from cheaper PLC-only institutes | Prove value with hardware hours, live demos and capstone projects; stackable programs let learners start at Rs 35,000 |
| **Capacity crunch** (from the model) | By Dec 2027 the 3 cells are about 100% utilised | Decide on cells 4–5 by Sep 2027; order early; stagger batch start dates |
