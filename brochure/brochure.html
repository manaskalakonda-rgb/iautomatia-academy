About the academy

We turn engineering graduates into industry-ready automation specialists

iAutomatia Academy is a hardware-first, job-oriented training institute. Every program is built around real industrial equipment, because plants hire people who can wire, program and troubleshoot machines, not people who have only used a simulator.

Hardware-first

Real JAKA cobots, real PLC trainer kits from three brands, real vision sensors and live industrial networks.

Small ratios

Batches of 12. At most 4 students share one cobot cell, so everyone gets regular hands-on time.

Job-oriented

Capstone project mentoring, interview preparation and placement support for Specialist learners.

The hardware lab

The same equipment you will meet on the plant floor

Each cobot cell combines a JAKA collaborative robot, a PLC, a vision sensor and grippers, connected over industrial networks — a small, complete production cell.

Image placeholderPHOTO: Wide shot of the lab — three cobot cells, PLC trainer benches and workstations

JAKA collaborative robots

Fence-less 6-axis cobots. Safety settings, jogging, teach pendant programming, tool frames, pick-and-place and integration with PLCs.

Three PLC brands

Siemens (TIA Portal), Mitsubishi (GX Works) and Allen-Bradley (Studio 5000) trainer kits with real I/O.

Vision sensors

Camera setup and calibration, part detection and vision-guided picking.

Industrial communication

Native Modbus TCP and Profinet links between PLC and cobot: handshakes, signal mapping and fault diagnosis.

Grippers & end-of-arm tooling

Grippers and EOAT selection, mounting, tool frames and payload settings.

HMI

Operator screens for start/stop, status and alarms, linked to the PLC.
