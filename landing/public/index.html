FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior PLC or robotics experience?

No. Foundation starts from the basics. Integrator and Specialist also begin with the Foundation content, so you can join any level as a beginner.

Which branches can join?

Mechanical, EEE, ECE, EIE and Mechatronics students and graduates are the best fit. Engineers from other branches with an interest in automation are welcome to attend a free demo first.

Is the training really on physical hardware?

Yes. You work on real 6-axis JAKA cobots, real Siemens, Mitsubishi and Allen-Bradley PLC trainer kits and real vision sensors. Software tools are used to program the hardware, not to replace it.

How much time will I get on the robot?

Batches have 12 students and at most 4 students share a cobot cell, so everyone gets regular hands-on time.

What are the batch timings?

Weekday batches run in the morning, afternoon or evening. Weekend batches are designed for working engineers. Contact us for the current schedule.

What are the fees? Is EMI available?

Foundation ₹35,000, Integrator ₹65,000, Specialist ₹95,000 (excluding GST). EMI options are available.

What certificate will I get?

You receive a certificate from iAutomatia Academy on successful completion. A co-branded OEM certificate is planned and not yet available.

Do you guarantee placement?

No. The Specialist program includes interview preparation and placement support, including introductions to hiring partners. Outcomes depend on your performance and the job market.
